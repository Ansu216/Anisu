The four Updates variants in one sentence each

  • 1 · Tight list — everything on one screen at the app's normal row height minus a little, nothing hidden, but smaller targets and nowhere for the update notes.
  • 2 · Status now, settings folded — the fastest to act on an update, the slowest to change a setting, and the only one where the screen can look empty.
  • 3 · Header actions — the fewest rows and the steadiest muscle memory, at the cost of unlabelled icons in the top bar.
  • 4 · Sticky action bar — the easiest to press, at the cost of a permanent 70dp band and a second coloured bar competing with the floating navigation bar.
